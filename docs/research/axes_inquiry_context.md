## Dissociation evidence already on record (the hard constraint for any unifier)
Paper 3 PROVES within-instruction clarity (81%) and between-instruction topology
(r=-0.274) co-vary INDEPENDENTLY — they measure different scales. So at minimum
the "single ε" must survive the fact that two of its candidate measurement scales
already empirically dissociate. A one-axis claim must explain why these dissociate
yet still be one axis, OR concede stratification.
